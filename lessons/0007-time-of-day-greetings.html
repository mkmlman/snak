Lesson 0007 Spoken Danish ~8 min

Time-of-day greetings

Godmorgen, godaften, and everything in between — match your greeting to the time like a Dane.

Today's win

Godmorgen (6–10) → Goddag (10–17) → Godaften (17–22) → Godnat (bedtime)

01 Time-of-day greetings

DanishEnglish
Godmorgen
Good morning (~6–10 AM)
Goddag
Good day (~10 AM–5 PM)
Godaften
Good evening (~5 PM–10 PM)
Godnat
Good night (bedtime / late)

02 Notes on usage

Cultural note

Godmorgen is very common at work or with neighbours in the first part of the day. Goddag is more formal — Hej is fine at any hour. Godaften is used when arriving somewhere in the evening. Godnat is only for leaving or sleeping.

The safe default at any hour is still Hej. But using the time-appropriate greeting signals that you're tuned into Danish social rhythm.

03 Related phrases

Hav en god dag

Have a good day

Standard send-off anywhere — shops, work, neighbours.

I lige måde

Likewise / same to you

Reply when someone wishes you a good day. Very common and expected.

Sov godt

Sleep well

What to say before bed, in person or in text.
