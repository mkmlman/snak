Lesson 0003 Spoken Danish ~12 min

How much? (Hvor meget + numbers 1–10)

Ask prices, count items, and understand numbers 1–10 — essential for shops, markets, and cafes.

Today's win

Hvor meget koster det? → Det koster 25 kroner.

01 Why this matters

Danes use cash less than most, but you still hear prices constantly. Knowing numbers 1–10 and Hvor meget? unlocks shops, cafes, and loppemarkeder (flea markets).

Pronunciation tip

syv sounds like "sew" (rhymes with "new"). Danish numbers use a unique counting system — just learn these 1–10 cold for now.

03 Phrase cards

Hvor meget koster det?

How much does it cost?

Word-by-word: hvor meget (how much) + koster (costs) + det (it) · Universal price question.

Det koster femogtyve kroner

It costs 25 kroner

Note: Danish prices use kroner (crowns). femogtyve = 25 (five-and-twenty). For now, just say the digits.

Hvor mange?

How many?

Use for countable items: Hvor mange øl? (How many beers?), Hvor mange kroner? (How many kroner?)

Kan jeg betale med kort?

Can I pay by card?

Denmark is near cashless. This is the standard checkout question.

Tak for handlen

Thanks for the purchase

You'll hear this from shop staff after paying. Reply with Selv tak.

04 Model dialogue

You
Hvor meget koster den her?

slownatural

How much does this one cost?

Shop assistant
Den koster 35 kroner.

slownatural

It costs 35 kroner.

You
Okay, jeg tager den. Kan jeg betale med kort?

slownatural

Okay, I'll take it. Can I pay by card?

Shop assistant
Ja, selvfølgelig. Tak for handlen.

slownatural

Yes, of course. Thanks for the purchase.

You
Selv tak!

slownatural

You're welcome!
