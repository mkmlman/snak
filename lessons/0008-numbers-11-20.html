Lesson 0008 Spoken Danish ~10 min

Numbers 11–20

Extend your numbers from 1–10 up to 20 — needed for prices, ages, quantities, and bus numbers.

Today's win

Elleve, tolv, tretten, fjorten, femten, seksten, sytten, atten, nitten, tyve — 11 through 20.

Pronunciation note

12 (tolv) is pronounced roughly "toll" — the v is silent. 20 (tyve) is "tew-eh". Danish numbers feel different from spelling — listen more than you read.

02 Pattern: the "-ten" suffix

Numbers 13–19 follow a consistent pattern: root + -ten (like English "-teen"). The exceptions are 11 (elleve) and 12 (tolv) — learn those as standalone words.

03 Phrases with numbers

Det koster 18 kroner

It costs 18 kroner

Practice combining numbers with prices.

Jeg er 20 år gammel

I am 20 years old

Pattern: [number] + år gammel = years old. Jeg er 17 år gammel (I'm 17).

Bus 14 går til Nørrebro

Bus 14 goes to Nørrebro

Bus numbers are said as individual numbers in Danish.

Before next lesson
  1. Count 11–20 out loud three times in Danish.
  2. Notice prices between 11–20 kroner on your next shop visit.
  3. Say elleve, tolv, tretten — those three are the most common in daily life.
