Polite neighbour check-in

Pocket sheet for Lesson 0001. Default register: du / dig.

Core loop

  1. Hej — hello
  2. Hvordan går det? — how are you?
  3. Det går godt — I'm fine
  4. Og dig? — and you?
  5. Hvad hedder du? — what's your name?
  6. Jeg hedder … — my name is …
  7. Tak — thank you
  8. Selv tak — you're welcome

Do / don't

Do: use du / dig with everyone — neighbours, staff, strangers.

Don't: worry about formal De — it's rarely used in modern Denmark.
