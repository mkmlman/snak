Lesson 0006 Spoken Danish ~8 min

Excuse me, help, sorry

Survival politeness — getting attention, apologising, and handling small awkward moments.

Today's win

Undskyld → Hjælp! → Det gør ikke noget — three phrases that cover 90% of awkward spots.

01 Phrase cards

Undskyld

Excuse me / sorry

The all-purpose word. Use for: getting attention, apologising (bumping into someone), or asking to pass. Pronounced "oon-sküll" roughly.

Beklager

Sorry (more serious / formal)

Undskyld is everyday; beklager is for when you actually mean it — being late, a mistake, bad news.

Hjælp!

Help!

Direct and clear in an emergency.

Kan du hjælpe mig?

Can you help me?

Polite ask — use in shops, on the street, at the station.

Det gør ikke noget

It doesn't matter / no worries

What to say when someone apologises to you. Very common and friendly.

Jeg forstår ikke

I don't understand

Useful when someone speaks too fast or you need them to switch to English.

Kan du sige det på engelsk?

Can you say it in English?

Almost all Danes speak excellent English. They'll switch happily.

02 Retrieval practice

1. "Excuse me / sorry" (everyday)
2. "It doesn't matter / no worries"
3. "Can you help me?"

03 Real-world task

Before next lesson
  1. Say Undskyld when passing someone in a narrow aisle or doorway.
  2. If someone apologises to you, reply Det gør ikke noget.
  3. Notice how often Danes say undskyld — it's everywhere.
