Lesson 0004 Spoken Danish ~10 min

Directions — left, right, straight

Give and understand simple directions on the street, in the metro, or at the train station.

Today's win

Undskyld, hvor er …? → Til venstre, til højre, lige ud.

01 Key vocab

DanishEnglish
til venstreto the left
til højreto the right
lige udstraight ahead
for enden af gadenat the end of the street
ved siden afnext to
over foropposite / across from

02 Phrase cards

Undskyld, hvor er …?

Excuse me, where is …?

Essential starter. Use with any place: … stationen? (the station), … Netto?, … toilettet? (the toilet).

Gå lige ud

Go straight ahead

Word-by-word: gå (walk) + lige (straight) + ud (out/ahead).

Drej til venstre / til højre

Turn left / right

Drej = turn. You'll also hear sving til venstre/højre (swing = turn, slightly more informal).

Det er derovre

It's over there

Vague but very common pointing phrase.

Tak for hjælpen

Thanks for the help

What to say after someone helps you with directions.

03 Model dialogue

You
Undskyld, hvor er stationen?
Excuse me, where is the station?

Stranger
Gå lige ud, og drej til højre for enden af gaden. Det er ved siden af Netto.
Go straight ahead, turn right at the end of the street. It's next to Netto.

You
Mange tak!
Thanks very much!

04 Retrieval practice

1. "To the left"
2. "Excuse me, where is the station?"
3. "Straight ahead"

05 Real-world task

Before next lesson
  1. Ask Undskyld, hvor er …? to someone on the street (try it once even if you know the way).
  2. Practice: til venstre, til højre, lige ud — say them while walking.
  3. Notice tak for hjælpen — listen for it after someone gives directions.
