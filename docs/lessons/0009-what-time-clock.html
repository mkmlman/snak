Lesson 0009 Spoken Danish ~12 min

What time? (clock time)

Ask and tell the time — for appointments, trains, and meeting up.

Today's win

Hvad er klokken? → Klokken er halv ti.

01 Key pattern

Pattern

Klokken er [number] = "The time is [number]."
Danes use the 12-hour clock conversationally but 24-hour for schedules.

02 Clock times

DanishEnglish
Klokken er etIt's 1 o'clock
Klokken er toIt's 2 o'clock
Klokken er halv toIt's half past one (1:30)
Klokken er kvart over toIt's quarter past two (2:15)
Klokken er kvart i treIt's quarter to three (2:45)
Klokken er fem over tiIt's five past ten (10:05)
Klokken er fem i halv toIt's five to half past one (1:25)
⚠ Half-hour trick

Danish halv to = half to two = 1:30 (halfway to two o'clock). This is the opposite of English "half past one."
Halv tre = 2:30, halv fire = 3:30, etc. Count forward to the next hour.

03 Phrase cards

Hvad er klokken?

What time is it?

The standard question. Klokken = the clock/watch.

Hvornår mødes vi?

When shall we meet?

Useful for making plans.

Vi ses klokken syv

See you at seven o'clock

Pattern: Vi ses (see you) + klokken + time.

Hvornår lukker I?

What time do you close?

Ask at shops, cafes, museums.

04 Retrieval practice

1. "What time is it?"
2. Danish "half past one" (1:30) is ...
3. "Quarter to three" (2:45)
4. "See you at seven"

05 Real-world task

Before next task
  1. Ask someone Hvad er klokken? (even if you're wearing a watch).
  2. Practice saying the time in Danish every time you check the clock today.
  3. Remember: halv to = 1:30, not 2:30!
