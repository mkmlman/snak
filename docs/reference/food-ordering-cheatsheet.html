Food ordering at a cafe

Pocket sheet for Lesson 0005.

Core pattern

Jeg vil gerne have + [item] — I would like [item].

Ordering flow

  1. Hej! Jeg vil gerne have en kaffe, tak. — Hi! I'd like a coffee, please.
  2. Staff: Værsgo. Skal det være noget andet? — Here you go. Anything else?
  3. Nej tak, det var det hele. — No thanks, that's all.
  4. Staff: Det bliver X kroner. — That'll be X kroner.
  5. Mange tak! God dag! — Thanks! Have a good day!
